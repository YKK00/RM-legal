Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the application RaiseToMemorize (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy very seriously. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

1. Overview of Data Collection

The App primarily stores data locally on your device (e.g., word memorization progress, timestamps of memorized words, local settings, selected vocabularies). This data will not be uploaded to our servers without your explicit consent.

2. Types of Data We May Collect or Process

  • Device and App Usage Information (Anonymous/Aggregated): To improve app functionality or troubleshoot crashes, we may collect anonymous usage statistics or crash logs (if you enable the "Error Reporting/Crash Reporting" feature). This information does not contain personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Local Data: Memorization records (including timestamps), selected vocabularies, and in-app settings. All of these are stored locally on your device.
  • Payment Information: If you use Apple's in-app purchases or subscriptions, all payment processing is handled by Apple. We cannot access or store your payment information or credit card numbers.

3. Use of Data

We process the aforementioned data solely for the following purposes: to provide and maintain app functionality; to save user progress and preferences (locally); to diagnose and fix errors using anonymous crash logs under user authorization; and to comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. If we use third-party services (such as crash analysis), we will truthfully declare them on the App Store Connect privacy page.

5. Data Storage and Security

The App prioritizes local storage; data is saved only on your device unless you actively enable cloud synchronization or other sharing features. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. User Rights

You can delete your local data by uninstalling the App or clearing records within the App. If you need us to delete any identifiable personal information we may hold (if any), please contact us using the contact information below.

7. Minors

The App is not designed for children under the age of 4. If you are a parent and discover that your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us so that we can delete that data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Please check this page regularly.

9. Contact Information
